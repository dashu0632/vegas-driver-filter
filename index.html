Backup and privacy

Settings and map polygons are stored in this browser's local storage. A new phone, a different browser, or private browsing will not automatically share them. Import/export saves your rules and boundaries. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap servers; city boundaries are fetched once from the Clark County public GIS service. No screenshot, ride data, or account credentials are sent by this application.

Safety and platform compliance: This app does not read Uber, Lyft, or delivery-app screens in the background. It does not draw over other apps, access driver accounts, accept/decline orders, or promise safe use while moving. Use only while parked. Check each platform's policies before using third-party analysis with its offers.

Source of North Las Vegas boundary: Clark County GIS Cities layer. City shapes may change; reload periodically.
